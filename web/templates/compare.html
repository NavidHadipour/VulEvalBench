Scoring method

Tools are ranked by a composite of three metrics, weighted as an explicit editorial choice rather than a hidden formula:

composite = {{ weights.f1 }} × F1 + {{ weights.tnr }} × (1 − FPR) + {{ weights.cwe }} × CWE accuracy

  • F1 — overall quality on the vulnerable class.
  • 1 − FPR — how far the tool can be trusted not to cry wolf. A detector that flags everything scores well on recall and is useless in practice; this term is what penalises it.
  • CWE accuracy — whether the tool understood what the flaw was, not merely that something was wrong.

The weights live in config.COMPOSITE_WEIGHTS and must sum to 1.0. Two further single-number summaries appear in the per-run tables: MCC, a correlation over the whole confusion matrix that cannot be inflated by always answering with the majority class, and Youden's J, which balances the two error types symmetrically.
